# Nexus: A Curated Archive for Us

Nexus is a premium, high-tech relationship companion designed to help partners track their shared lives, capture memories, and visualize their journey together. Inspired by the minimalist and sophisticated design language of **Nothing**, Nexus combines a "dot-matrix" aesthetic with immersive interactive elements.

## 🚀 Core Features

### 1. Immersive Memory Board (Archive)
*   **Apple Watch-Inspired Bubble Grid**: A fluid, draggable hexagonal grid where memories are showcased as interactive bubbles.
*   **Fisheye Scaling**: Items near the center of the screen scale up and gain color, while those at the edges shrink and fade into grayscale, creating a focused exploration experience.
*   **Dashboard Integration**: The grid includes "Stat Bubbles" like total memory counts and last sync times, making it a living dashboard.
*   **Full-Screen Detail View**: Dive into high-resolution images and detailed descriptions of your captured moments.

### 2. Intelligent Life Tracking (Log Data)
*   **Specialized Inputs**:
    *   **Mood**: Visual emoji-based selector to track emotional trends.
    *   **Activity**: Quick-select chips for common activities or custom entries.
    *   **Food**: Dedicated logging for shared meals and drinks.
    *   **Health**: Descriptive status tracking for physical well-being.
*   **Advanced Money Splitter**: Enter a total bill amount and use a slider to calculate shares based on a custom ratio (defaulting to 50/50).

### 3. Data Analytics
*   **Mood Trends**: Visualize emotional highs and lows over time with smooth line charts.
*   **Expense History**: Track shared spending with area charts that respect your chosen currency.
*   **Activity Distribution**: See how you spend your time together with category-based bar charts.

### 4. Global Configuration
*   **Currency Support**: Full support for USD, EUR, GBP, INR, and JPY.
*   **Timezone Sync**: Configure your preferred timezone (e.g., IST, UTC) for accurate data logging.
*   **Partner Profiles**: Toggle between partners to log data individually but view it collectively.

## 🎨 Design Philosophy
*   **Nothing-Inspired**: A deep black canvas, glassmorphic surfaces, and a vibrant purple accent (`#a855f7`).
*   **Bento & Bubble Grids**: Modern layout patterns that balance information density with visual elegance.
*   **Motion First**: Powered by `framer-motion` for weighted panning, spring-based animations, and smooth transitions.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack
*   **Frontend**: React 18+, Vite, Tailwind CSS.
*   **Backend**: Node.js, Express.
*   **Database**: SQLite (via `better-sqlite3`) for robust local data persistence.
*   **Visualization**: Recharts for high-performance data visualization.
*   **Icons**: Lucide React for a consistent, crisp icon set.

## 📖 How to Use
1.  **Dashboard**: Get a high-level overview of your latest metrics and recent memories.
2.  **Archive**: Drag and pan the bubble grid to explore your shared history. Use the `+` button to capture a new memory.
3.  **Log Data**: Use the specialized forms to sync your daily metrics.
4.  **Analytics**: Visit the analytics tab to see the "big picture" of your relationship data.
5.  **Settings**: Configure your currency and timezone to tailor the app to your location.
